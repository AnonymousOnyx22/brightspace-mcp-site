Questions

Does my data go to your servers?

No. Pro runs on your computer and signs in exactly like the free version. Our server only checks with Stripe that your subscription is active. It never sees your Brightspace sign-in, grades or courses.

Does my computer need to be on?

Yes. Alerts come from your computer, so they arrive while it's on and you're logged in. Pro starts by itself every time you log in.

Which computers does it work on?

Windows, Mac and Linux, anywhere the free version works.

Can it change anything in Brightspace?

No. It only reads. It can't submit, post or change anything.

How do I cancel?

Use "Manage your subscription" on this page with your Pro key, or the link on the page you saw after paying. You keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for.
