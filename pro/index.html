Questions

How is my data handled in Pro?

Brightspace gives the app a sign-in token, which is stored encrypted. Your grades and course content are fetched when you ask a question and passed to your AI app, but not saved on our server. You can disconnect and delete everything at any time. Full details are in the privacy policy.

Can it change anything in Brightspace?

No. Like the free version, it only reads. It can't submit, post or change anything.

Why does Pro cost money?

It runs on a server that has to be paid for and kept secure. The free version costs nothing because it runs on your own computer.
