How to start

  1. Start your free trial. Checkout is handled by Stripe. You won't be charged for 7 days, and you can cancel before then.
  2. Run setup. The page after checkout gives you a one-click setup file for Windows, or one command for Mac and Linux, with your key already in it.
  3. Sign in and ask. Sign in to Brightspace the way you always do. Then ask your AI "What should I work on right now?"

Manage your subscription

Questions

Will I be charged during the trial?

No. Your card is charged $4 CAD only after 7 days. Cancel before then and you pay nothing.

Does my data go to your servers?

No. The app runs on your computer and signs in through your school's own page. Our server only checks with Stripe that your subscription is active.

Does my computer need to be on for alerts?

Yes. Alerts come from your computer, so they arrive while it's on. They start by themselves every time you log in.

Which computers does it work on?

Windows, Mac and Linux, with Node.js 20 or newer and Chrome or Edge.

Can it change anything in Brightspace?

No. It only reads. It can't submit, post or change anything.

How do I cancel?

Use "Manage your subscription" above with your key. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
