Why the to-do list matters

Due dates alone don't tell you what's left. Brightspace MCP checks your actual submission for every assignment, so your AI never tells you to do work you already handed in.

Other options

Hosted integration platforms such as Pipedream also offer D2L Brightspace connectors, and the R package brightspaceR includes an MCP setup aimed at data analysis. Those suit different needs than a student's personal assistant.
