Privacy policy

Short version: the free version never sends your school data to us. Pro stores only what it needs to sign you in, and never sells anything.

Last updated October 13, 2026

Who we are

Brightspace D2L MCP Server ("the project", "we") is an independent project by a student developer, published at brightspacemcp.com and on GitHub. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by D2L Corporation.

The free version

The free version runs entirely on your own computer. It signs in to Brightspace through a normal browser window, and your session stays on your computer. Your courses, grades and files go from Brightspace straight to your computer and to the AI app you choose. None of it is sent to us.

The only outside request the free version makes on its own is a check of the public npm registry to see whether a newer version exists. That request doesn't include any personal or school information.

The AI app you connect (for example Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor) receives the answers it asks for. That company's own privacy policy covers what it does with them.

This website

The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We don't use advertising trackers or sell any data. If you join the Pro waitlist, we collect your email address and school name, and use them only to tell you when Pro is available. Ask us to delete them anytime.

Pro (hosted version)

Pro runs on our server so you don't have to install anything. When you use it:

  • Sign-in token. When you sign in with Brightspace, your school gives the app an access token. We store it encrypted and use it only to read your Brightspace data when you ask a question. We never see or store your password.
  • Your course data. Grades, assignments and course content are fetched when your AI app asks for them and passed straight back to it. We don't keep copies.
  • Account details. Your email address and your school, so we can run your account and contact you about it.
  • Payments. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe's privacy policy covers the payment details they handle.
  • Logs. Basic technical logs (time, request type, errors) to keep the service working and secure. They don't include your grades or course content. They're kept for up to 30 days.

Pro data is stored on servers in Canada.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Pro at any time. Your stored token is deleted right away.
  • Cancel your subscription at any time from the billing page.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account and everything linked to it. We'll do it within 30 days.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent personal information. We share it only with the services needed to run the project (Netlify for hosting, Stripe for payments, and our server provider), or when required by law.

Security

Tokens are encrypted at rest, all traffic uses HTTPS, and the app can only read from Brightspace, never write. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever affects your information we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

The project is meant for college and university students. It isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Significant changes will be announced to Pro users by email before they take effect.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@brightspacemcp.com. You can also open an issue on GitHub for anything that isn't private.

If you're in Canada and aren't satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
